Sehri Dua (Arabic):
وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَّوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ

Sehri Dua (English):
I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow in the month of Ramadan.

Iftar Dua (Arabic):
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ

Iftar Dua (English):
O Allah! I fasted for You and I believe in You.
